Fine for me

Traps the checker always looks for. Japanese and Asian: regular soy sauce, oyster and hoisin sauce, gochujang, kanikama, tempura and "crunchy" bits, teriyaki, eel sauce, ponzu, wheat noodles. Arabic and Levantine: bulgur in tabbouleh and kibbeh, freekeh, pita croutons, vermicelli rice, shawarma marinades, falafel binders. Indian: hing (asafoetida, often cut with wheat), naan, samosa, batter. Western: roux sauces, gravy, flour tortillas, beer batter, malt vinegar. Plus quinoa and oats everywhere, including granola, porridge and "ancient grain" bowls.

Every listing comes from menus, reviews and forums, and any of them can be out of date. "Restaurant marks GF" is the strongest evidence here, and it is still not a guarantee: confirm with the restaurant before you order.
